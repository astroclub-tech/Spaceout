What is SPACEOUT?

SPACEOUT is an Astro Ideathon: a place where curious minds, dreamers and future innovators explore space, astronomy and space science through creative thinking, hands-on activities and collaborative ideation.

Our purpose

To inspire fresh ideas in astronomy and space science, and to help you think beyond boundaries and turn curiosity into meaningful solutions.

The journey

Four steps from idea to the final round.

Register

Sign up alone or form a team of up to 4 members.

Create your idea

Develop your space-related idea and submit it as a PPT or PDF before the deadline.

Evaluation

Ideas are judged on innovation, creativity, relevance and problem-solving potential.

Offline final

Selected participants pitch at GLA University, Mathura.

Themes & problem statements

Six themes, one mission. Choose a problem statement and build your idea around it.

THEME 01

Space Infrastructure & Safety

Keeping satellites themselves safe and operational

  • Space Debris Detection & Collision Avoidance AI
  • Space Debris Collision Early-Warning System for Small Satellites/CubeSats
  • LEO/MEO Satellite Communication Optimizer
  • Multi-Satellite Data Fusion Dashboard
THEME 02

Space Weather & Its Impact on Earth

Managing disruptions caused by solar and space weather activity

  • Space Weather Monitoring & Solar Storm Risk Predictor
  • Solar Storm Impact Predictor for Power Grids
  • Space Weather Alert System for Farmers & Telecom Operators
THEME 03

Deep Space Science & Discovery

Understanding the universe: core astronomy and research

  • Automated Exoplanet Habitability Scoring System
THEME 04

Earth Monitoring for Disaster & Environment

Using satellite and drone technology to protect Earth's environment and safety

  • Satellite-Based Real-Time Natural Disaster Prediction
  • Forest Fire Detection Using Satellite & Drone Imagery
  • Cloud-Native Land-Use Change Detection System
  • Light Pollution Reduction Advisory System
THEME 05

Applied Drone Technology

Ground-level, drone-specific practical applications

  • AI-Based Border Surveillance Using Drones
  • AWS-Powered Drone Navigation
  • Precision Agriculture Analytics Using Drone Imagery
THEME 06

Open Innovation

Any original, tech-driven solution beyond the above

  • Open Innovation: bring your own problem statement
